# Learn the resolution problem

Begin with the [explanatory report (PDF)](report/Markovian_Resolution_Limit_Report.pdf) for a continuous account from quantum states, measurement and open-system dynamics to the intervention law and its sensing consequences. It introduces the needed mathematics as the argument develops, with worked examples and an exact echo diagram.

Both sister projects use **Rivas and Huelga, _Open Quantum Systems: An Introduction_, arXiv:1104.5242v2**, as their single common educational anchor. The [source bridge](ANCHOR.md) identifies the relevant sections and translates their conventions. Reading the whole source first is unnecessary.

| Purpose | Route |
|---|---|
| Follow a concrete experiment | [Worked tutorial](TUTORIAL.md): two models with the same final decay channel respond differently to an intermediate pulse |
| Build the conceptual foundations | M01–M11 in the [30-milestone map](MILESTONES.md): states, channels, environments, interventions and statistical tasks |

| Follow the sister's distinct question | [Noise Sensing learning guide](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Noise-Rate-Activated-Sensing/blob/main/learning/README.md): how coupling selection changes estimation precision |

The report explains the connected argument; the tutorial develops selected examples; the scientific pages retain the canonical statements, complete proofs and attribution. The milestone map is common to every reader. The shared recorded-phase method has its principal exposition in the sister repository, while the contact-budget and timing theorems are developed here.

The report's editable source and compilation instructions are in the [technical build guide](../verification/README.md#explanatory-report-build).
